Hi! I'm Calico. Game Development is very close to my heart. The challenge of creating an immersive experience and to make something innovative is something that I strive for constantly. I specialize in Systems Design, and the technical aspects of game development. Problem solving is something I enjoy greatly, and excel at.

There is an unspoken, subtle, but very noticable quality of the underlying mechanisms of a game that affect how players experience the game. My goal is to understand and create the best mechanisms for both players, and for designers who will use my systems.

I also enjoy and am experienced with narrative design, and gameplay design. I hope to one day be part of a project that does something new and memorable.

Cold Fusion - Solo Project

March 2025 - May 2025

16:9 Gameplay Image

Through the course of 10 weeks, I created the gameplay prototype for Cold Fusion, a 3D shoot-'em-up inside of Unreal Engine. I implemented complex systems such as dynamic Object Pooling, and constructed my systems in a way that a gameplay designer could easily use them, anticipating further development of the project in a team environment and to allow for rapid development of levels in the future. 

Overgrown! - Systems Design and UI Implementation

Fall 2024 - Winter 2025

16:9 Gameplay Image

Working on the first two out of the three quarters that Overgrown! was set for development, I worked with a large team of other systems designers, including a more experienced systems design lead and followed the design patterns and principles set out for the larger project. I developed systems that would be used by gameplay designers, and would be flexible enough to be expanded upon by other systems designers who would use the system after me. I then thoroughly documented these systems for later use.

Supercritical - Team Lead and Game Design

Fall 2024 - Winter 2025

16:9 Gameplay Image

Supercritical is one of my earliest works, which I developed along with a team of four other designers. We had six weeks to prototype, develop, and produce a physical copy of a four player cooperative board game that would play for up to 60 minutes. I acted as team lead, managing the asset list, task distribution, and scope decisions. I was also in charge of creating the core gameplay and narrative. Challenges included time management for the creation of assets, frequent playtesting within the class, and physically producing the game pieces; including printing locally, cutting, assembling ourselves.
