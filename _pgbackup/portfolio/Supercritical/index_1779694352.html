Supercritical

Following a catastrophic solar flare, descend to the surface of post-apocalyptic venus to scavange for resources and keep your low-orbit station from going supercritical.

Download Rulebook
Supercritical is a cooperative strategy game where four players must work together to complete objectives across the surface of Venus, while managing resources to acquire upgrades and keep the reactor stable. At the start of each objective, each players must choose a character to deploy as a Commander and a Field Team Member. Players should strategize and work together to cover eachother's weaknesses whilst bringing their best team members for the given objectives they're setting out to complete.
Supercritical focuses heavily on resource management and team decision making. Players travel together across the surface of venus in their rover, and have a limited number of resources that each can carry. Players can exchange resources, gear, and survivors with eachother, which encourages communication and cooperation. Players progress by acquiring new survivors, new resources, and most notably new Gear which can be equipped on either their Field Team members, or on their Commanders.
